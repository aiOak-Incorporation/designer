About designer.aioak.co

designer.aioak.co is a community-driven platform that showcases designers, their work, and events — helping creatives connect, collaborate, and grow. The site makes it simple to discover designers, browse collections, and reach out for opportunities.

Purpose

Our purpose is to amplify designers by giving them streamlined tools to present their portfolios, share collections, list events, and be discovered by brands, collaborators, and clients.

How to Use

  • Explore Designers: Browse the Designers page to find talent.
  • View Collections: Dive into recent and past work.
  • Check Events: Discover showcases, pop-ups, and runway shows.
  • Contact: Reach out directly to collaborate or hire.
  • Join: Designers can sign up and manage their profiles and designs.

Collaboration

We encourage collaboration between designers — from co-created capsules to shared runway moments. The platform helps you find peers with complementary skills and aesthetics.

Global Reach

Connect with designers from all over the world — spanning multiple regions and at least 12 countries — and build relationships that cross borders and cultures.
