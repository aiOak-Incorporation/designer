I’m Manya Reddy Marri, a fashion professional and graduate of Arizona State University (ASU), with a degree in Fashion. Currently, I work as a Production Manager at Emerald Sundae in Los Angeles, California, where I help manage the full product lifecycle — from design development and sourcing to production, quality assurance, and delivery.

With a background that bridges both design and production, I bring a unique perspective that balances creativity, craftsmanship, and operational efficiency. My journey in fashion has been fueled by innovation, attention to detail, and a passion for translating concepts into high-quality, market-ready garments.

Experience

I aim to collaborate with forward-thinking brands, designers, and creatives who value innovation, sustainability, and craftsmanship in fashion.
